People come to RezScore with two related questions: "Which jobs fit my background?" and "How should I change my resume for this one?"

Job Matcher handles both. You can bring a posting and target your resume to it, or ask RezScore to find real openings that fit your experience first.

## Two ways to start

There are two halves, and we want to be clear about which is which.

**Bring a job posting.** Paste the description and RezScore prepares targeted changes to your resume. You review the changes before applying them, and your original stays untouched. Your first targeted resume is free.

**Let RezScore find the roles.** RezScore reads the career signals already in your resume, searches current openings, and ranks the results. Choose one and you can create a version of your resume targeted to that role.

Searching for roles is included with Professional and Premium plans.

## Three ways to use it

**Free.** Bring one job description and create your first targeted resume. This is the direct route when you already know which role you want.

**Professional.** Ask Jen to find openings that match your resume. The results appear in chat, where you can open the real posting and target your resume to it.

**Premium.** Use the job-search dashboard to refresh your matches, compare openings, and keep each targeted resume with its source posting.

Real openings come from Adzuna. Every result we show has a link to the source posting. If a listing has no real application link, it never becomes a match.

## How the matching works

The search begins with facts already in your resume: your recent job title, professional headline, industry, location, skills, and years of experience. Those signals become a set of job searches, starting specific and widening when a narrow search returns nothing.

RezScore then ranks the returned openings using three things it can explain: keyword overlap, industry fit, and seniority fit. The ranking does not ask an AI model to invent a career path or make up a role. It compares the text of a real posting with the evidence in your resume.

That makes the result useful, not prophetic. A high match means the posting lines up with the experience your resume shows. It cannot know whether the company has an internal candidate, how a recruiter will weigh a career change, or whether a listing will close tomorrow.

## How the tailoring works

Once you choose a posting, RezScore prepares changes against that job description. You review the proposed wording before it reaches your resume. Keep a change, skip it, or leave the original alone.

Two rules matter:

- **We never make jobs up.** Every surfaced role comes from a real listing with a source link. If the search returns nothing useful, we say so.
- **We never make you up.** Tailoring should sharpen and reorganize what is already true. It should not give you a skill you do not have or a result you did not earn. You remain the final reviewer.
